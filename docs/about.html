Biography

Yifan Hu is an Assistant Professor at Rutgers University in New Brunswick, New Jersey. His research spans optimization, learning, operations research, and statistics, with a focus on decision making under uncertainty. He develops models and simple methods with provable guarantees.

Before joining Rutgers, he was a postdoctoral researcher at EPFL and ETH Zurich. He earned his PhD in Operations Research from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and a bachelor's degree in mathematics from Nankai University.

Outside research, he enjoys skiing, swimming, hiking, and tennis.
