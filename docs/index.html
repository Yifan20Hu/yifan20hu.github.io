Assistant Professor of Statistics

Department of Statistics · Rutgers University
New Brunswick, New Jersey

I study decision making under uncertainty at the intersection of optimization, reinforcement learning, statistics, and causality.

My goal is to build new models and simple methods with provable guarantees. Before joining Rutgers, I was a postdoctoral researcher at EPFL and ETH Zurich. I earned a PhD in Operations Research from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and a bachelor's degree in mathematics from Nankai University.
