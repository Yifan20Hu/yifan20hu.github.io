02 / UPDATES

Latest News.

Research, talks, and milestones from the group.

  1. Oct 2026

    Two new preprints are out: PRICE allocates test-time rollouts and voting under a token budget, while Dr. OPD learns which teacher signals most help a student language model.

  2. Sep 2026

    Our PRICE paper was selected for an oral presentation at the NeurIPS 2026 FLLMPT Workshop and accepted by the MLxOR, TTCL, and RAAAI workshops.

  3. Jun 2026

    A new preprint with Mengmeng Li, Daniel Kuhn, and Yan Li develops sample-efficient methods for robust Markov decision processes with continuous state spaces.

  4. Apr 2026

    A new preprint with Buse Şen and Daniel Kuhn introduces multistage conditional compositional optimization and reduces the scenario cost of nested decisions.

  5. Jan 2026

    I received the 2025 Mathematical Programming Meritorious Service Award.
