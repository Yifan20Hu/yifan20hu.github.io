Data Mining.

16:960:588 · Rutgers University · Fall 2026

An introduction to modern machine learning and data mining with an emphasis on probabilistic foundations.

Topics

Probabilistic modeling, uncertainty quantification, deep learning, generative modeling, representation learning, and AI-assisted workflows.

Assessment

  • Midterm examination: 40%.
  • Group project: 60%. Groups of up to four submit a technical blog post and present their work.
  • Homework: up to 20% bonus.

The October 19 midterm has a two-hour closed-book component followed by a one-hour LLM-supported problem-solving component. LLM use is prohibited in the closed-book component and must be disclosed when used in the final hour. The project emphasizes understanding, obstacles, and milestones rather than requiring a completely solved problem.
