Data Mining.

16:960:588 · Rutgers University · Fall 2026

An introduction to modern machine learning and data mining, grounded in the probabilistic methods behind contemporary AI.

Course Focus

Students study probabilistic modeling, uncertainty quantification, deep learning, generative models, and representation learning. The course connects mathematical and statistical principles to generative AI, multimodal learning, and foundation models. It also develops critical, effective use of AI tools in problem solving.

Assessment

  • Midterm examination: 40%.
  • Course project: 60%.
  • Bonus homework: up to 20%.

The October 19 midterm starts with a two-hour closed-book written examination. A one-hour LLM-supported problem-solving portion follows, allowing students to revisit unsolved questions and tackle new ones. LLM use is required as part of the course, prohibited during the closed-book portion, permitted in the final hour, and must be disclosed whenever used.

Course Project

Teams of up to four use modern AI tools for a substantial technical project, such as investigating a research question or building a data-to-decisions pipeline. Evaluation emphasizes understanding the problem, identifying obstacles, and reaching concrete milestones rather than fully solving the question. The final written deliverable is a technical blog post, with project presentations at the end of the term.

Bonus homework offers challenging questions for students to explore with any suitable methods.
