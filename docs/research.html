DIRECTION 0115 works

Stochastic Optimization

Global structure and biased gradients

I study when hidden convexity, convex recursions, or other structure make global solutions possible in seemingly nonconvex decision problems. I also develop stochastic methods for bilevel, compositional, and multistage optimization when gradient estimates are biased or costly.

Structured nonconvexity

Global guarantees from hidden convexity and benign landscapes in policy and causal optimization.

DIRECTION 025 works

Reinforcement Learning

Robust and aligned decisions

I develop methods for sequential decisions that remain reliable under model uncertainty. This includes policy optimization landscapes, robust Markov decision processes, and systems in which an agent’s incentives must align with a designer’s goal.

Policy structure

Understand when nonconvex policy methods reach globally good decisions.

Robustness and incentives

Design contextual bilevel methods, robust control, and preference learning under uncertainty.

DIRECTION 034 works

Causality & Statistical Learning

Inference with tractable structure

Causal questions and distribution shifts often lead to difficult estimation problems. I formulate them as optimization problems whose structure supports efficient computation, statistical guarantees, and fairer learning across groups.

Optimization for Causality

Model causal invariance learning for causal discovery and causal inference via instrumental variables as optimization problems.

Robust Inference and Fairness

Study group distributional robustness, reliable statistical inference, and fairness in federated learning.
